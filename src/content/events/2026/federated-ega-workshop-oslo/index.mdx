ELIXIR Norway invites you to a one-day workshop on managing and sharing human sensitive data, with a particular focus on the [Federated European Genome-phenome Archive (FEGA)](https://ega-archive.org/about/projects-and-funders/federated-ega/) and the [FEGA Norway](https://ega.elixir.no/) node operated by ELIXIR Norway.

## Morning seminar (10:00-12:00)

Funders and scientific best practice mandate that research data should be open; that is, as open as possible, as closed as necessary. The [European Health Data Space (EHDS)](https://health.ec.europa.eu/ehealth-digital-health-and-care/european-health-data-space_en) regulation makes this best practice law for health research data, as all such data, along with other health data, should be made available for use in research from 2031.

We will present the Federated EGA network and the FEGA Norway node, which are the European solution for archiving genomic research data according to current best practices. We will also present upcoming infrastructure initiatives for human sensitive data, including the [1+ Million Genomes](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/1-million-genomes) initiative, the [Genomic Data Infrastructure (GDI)](https://gdi.onemilliongenomes.eu/) project, and the [EOSC-ENTRUST](https://eosc-entrust.eu/) blueprint for federated sensitive data research, and describe how these relate to and support EHDS.

*Lunch break (12:00-13:00)*

## Afternoon hands-on session (13:00-15:00)

Participants will learn how the Federated EGA metadata model is structured and will go through a complete data submission to FEGA Norway, including preparing the required encrypted files and accompanying metadata.

## Who should attend?

Project PIs, researchers, data managers and support staff who handle or plan to submit human sensitive data, such as human genomics and other human-related data.
