[The Nordic Data Stewardship Network (NDSN)](https://datice.is/en/nordic-data-stewardship-network) had its second annual meeting in Finland at [the IT Centre for Science (CSC)](https://csc.fi/en/). Dr [Federico Bianchini](https://www.mn.uio.no/bils/english/people/fredebi/),
Senior Engineer at the Department of Biosciences at the University of Oslo (UiO) and long-time member of ELIXIR Norway,
is part of the steering group of NDSN and contributed to the event with a presentation on the work on tooling for Data Management Planning done by ELIXIR Norway in collaboration with the BOTT University Libraries and SIKT. Moreover, Bianchini was invited to join the panel of experts for the panel session at the end of the meeting.

The event was successful, with 120 registered participants (of which 40 attended in person).
There was a good participation from ELIXIR, with members from Norway, Finland, and Estonia present on-site.
You can read more about the event and access all the presentations [here](https://kannu.csc.fi/s/yTC7MXtCQ6HbjfG?dir=/&editing=false&openfile=true).
