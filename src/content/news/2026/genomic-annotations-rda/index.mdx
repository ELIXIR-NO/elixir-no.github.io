Dr Sveinung Gundersen (University of Oslo - ELIXIR Norway) is giving a talk on the aims and preliminary results of the “FAIRification of Genomic Annotations WG” in RDA, which is nearing completion.
Gundersen will also reflect on the broader process of forming and running a domain‑specific RDA Working Group. He will share experiences with proposal development, international coordination, stakeholder engagement, and navigating the RDA ecosystem, including our experience as a pilot WG of the RDA TIGER project. The talk will highlight what has worked well, what has been challenging, and what might be useful for others considering similar initiatives.

Dr Sveinung Gundersen is a Senior Engineer based at the Department of Biosciences at the University of Oslo. He holds a PhD in Bioinformatics and an MTech in Computer Science, specialising in statistical functional genomics and FAIR data infrastructure. He is currently developing Omnipy, a Python package for automating complex data integration and enforcing FAIR standards across research pipelines. Sveinung has been a member of ELIXIR Norway since the very beginning of the project in 2014.

The talk will be streamed on August 28th at 9:30 CEST as part of the "Research Data Insider" series organised by the "RDA in Norway" Regional RDA Group.
More information and streaming details can be found in [this post in the group's forum](https://www.rd-alliance.org/groups/rda-norway/posts/reminder-webinar-august-28th-0930-1000-the-research-data-insider-fairification-of-genomic-annotations-lessons-from-establishing-an-rda-working-group/).
