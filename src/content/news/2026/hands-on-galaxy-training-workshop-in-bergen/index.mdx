ELIXIR Norway invites life science researchers to a hands-on [Galaxy](/services/galaxy) workshop at the University of Bergen on Thursday 12 November 2026, from 09:00 to 16:00. The workshop takes place in the Stort auditorium at Høyteknologisenteret.

Galaxy is an open, web-based platform for data-intensive biology, and [usegalaxy.no](https://usegalaxy.no) is the national Galaxy instance, operated as part of the Norwegian e-infrastructure for life sciences. During the workshop, participants will learn to run analyses and build reusable workflows on usegalaxy.no, with guidance from experienced instructors.

No programming experience is needed. The workshop is aimed at life science researchers who want to get started with reproducible, workflow-based analysis of their data. Lunch is provided.

Registration closes on 1 November 2026. To register, or if you have any questions, contact us at [training@elixir.no](mailto:training@elixir.no).
