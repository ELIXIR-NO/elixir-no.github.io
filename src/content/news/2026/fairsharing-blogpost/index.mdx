The work of Dr Federico Bianchini (University of Oslo - ELIXIR Norway) was recently showcased as part of the spotlight series on FAIRsharing Blog highlighting the contributions of FAIRsharing Communities Champions.
Bianchini joined the [FAIRsharing Community Champions Programme](https://fairsharing.org/community_champions/) in 2025, as the representative for the [ELIXIR Research Data Management Community](https://elixir-europe.org/communities/research-data-management).

Bianchini led a BioHackathon project in 2025, [Towards a more scalable, sustainable, and integrated Research Data Management Ecosystem](https://github.com/elixir-europe/biohackathon-projects-2025/blob/main/27.md).
As part of this activity, the technical teams of RDMkit and FAIRsharing worked together to develop a Python-based integration that acts as a bridge between the two resources: this script automatically creates and updates [FAIRsharing collections](https://fairsharing.org/search?fairsharingRegistry=Collection&q=RDMkit) based on the content of RDMkit's [Your domain pages](https://rdmkit.elixir-europe.org/your_domain).

Bianchini is part of the editorial board of RDMkit and has been working on this service since his involvement in ELIXIR CONVERGE in early 2021.
