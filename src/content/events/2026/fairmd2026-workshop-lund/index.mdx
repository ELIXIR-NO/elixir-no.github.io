From 7 to 10 September 2026, the FAIRMD2026 Workshop brought researchers from across the molecular modelling community to the [LINXS Institute of advanced Neutron and X-ray Science](https://www.linxs.se/) in Lund, close to the [European Spallation Source (ESS)](https://ess.eu/). The workshop was organised by [VTT](https://www.vttresearch.com/), the [University of Bergen (UiB)](https://www.uib.no/) and ELIXIR Norway, and was enabled by the FAIRMD – Disorder to Order project, funded through the EU [OSCARS](https://oscars-project.eu/) project. LINXS partnered with FAIRMD to host the event.

Over four days of hands-on collaboration, participants from several countries worked on advancing the FAIRMD Lipids and IDPs Databanks. The focus was on verifying molecular dynamics (MD) simulations of lipids and intrinsically disordered proteins (IDPs) against small-angle X-ray scattering (SAXS) and nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) experiments, tackling concrete challenges together, and exploring how high-quality, [FAIR](https://www.go-fair.org/fair-principles/) simulation data can support new applications, including machine learning models for SAXS.

A big thank you to all participants for bringing their expertise, ideas and collaborative spirit to the workshop, and to LINXS for providing an inspiring environment. We look forward to seeing how the work continues to develop.

The FAIRMD Lipids Databank is developed openly by the [NMRlipids](https://nmrlipids.github.io/) community; the code is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/NMRLipids/FAIRMD_lipids).
