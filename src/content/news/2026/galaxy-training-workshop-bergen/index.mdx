ELIXIR Norway is hosting a hands-on [Galaxy](/services/galaxy) training workshop in Bergen on 12 November 2026. The workshop will introduce participants to the Norwegian Galaxy platform at [usegalaxy.no](https://usegalaxy.no), covering the basics of running analyses, building reusable workflows, and managing data in a reproducible way — all through a graphical web interface, with no programming required.

The workshop is open to researchers and students from all life science disciplines, and no prior experience with Galaxy is needed — just a laptop and a web browser. It is a great opportunity to see how ELIXIR Norway's e-infrastructure can support your own analyses, and our trainers will be on hand throughout the day to help you get started.
