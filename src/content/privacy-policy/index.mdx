This privacy policy describes how ELIXIR Norway collects and processes data about visitors to this website, elixir.no.

## About this website

This is the website of ELIXIR Norway, the Norwegian node of [ELIXIR](https://elixir-europe.org/), the European infrastructure for life science data.

The data collected through this website is processed under a joint controllership arrangement established pursuant to the Consortium Agreement for ELIXIR4OLS, Enabling Open Life Science in Norway ("the Consortium").

The participating institutions acting as joint controllers are:

- University of Bergen (UiB), Department of Informatics (lead partner)
- Norwegian University of Life Sciences (NMBU) (lead partner)
- Norwegian University of Science and Technology (NTNU) (lead partner)
- University of Oslo (UiO) (lead partner)
- UiT The Arctic University of Norway (UiT) (lead partner)
- Norwegian Institute of Public Health (FHI) (additional partner)
- Norwegian Veterinary Institute (additional partner)

These institutions jointly determine the purposes and means of processing the data, in accordance with the Consortium Agreement and applicable data protection legislation.

## Legal basis for the processing

Personal data is processed on the basis of the Consortium's legitimate interests, as provided for under applicable data protection legislation.

These legitimate interests include monitoring and evaluating how the website is used, assessing service performance, maintaining the security and functionality of the website, fulfilling reporting and accountability obligations, and continuously improving and developing the services we provide. The purposes are described in more detail under "Purpose of the processing" below.

Because Matomo stores cookies in your browser, it runs only after you accept it. You can change your choice at any time, as described under "Your choice" below.

## How we collect your data

This website uses [Matomo](https://matomo.org/), an open-source web analytics platform. Web analytics tools let website operators measure, collect, analyse and report on how a website is used and how visitors interact with it. This supports monitoring of service performance, evaluation of user engagement, and identification of opportunities for improvement.

We use Matomo to produce statistics about the use of this website, which help us maintain, improve and develop it. Matomo is configured to collect as little as possible:

- It runs only if you accept analytics. Until you do, it records nothing and sets no cookies.
- It honours Do Not Track. If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, Matomo records nothing about your visits.

Where Matomo is hosted is described under "Transfers to third countries" below. A demonstration of Matomo and its reports is available at [demo.matomo.cloud](https://demo.matomo.cloud).

## Purpose of the processing

Analytics data from this website serves several legitimate operational and reporting purposes. In particular, it is used to establish and monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), including metrics on how much the website is used and how visitors reach it. Collecting this information is a requirement for ELIXIR Norway services and supports institutional reporting, performance assessment, and compliance with funding and governance obligations.

Analytics data is also used to understand how visitors use the website and to find opportunities for improvement. This includes detecting and analysing broken links, "page not found" errors, search engine indexing problems, and other issues that affect usability or discoverability. Aggregated usage statistics also help us assess how relevant and engaging our content is.

Matomo turns the collected data into statistical reports that help the website administrators evaluate the website and its user experience. These reports may inform decisions about design, navigation, accessibility, content and other improvements to the quality and usability of the website.

The collected data is never used for unrelated purposes, including marketing, advertising, profiling, behavioural targeting, or automated decision-making. The Consortium does not use analytics data to make decisions that produce legal effects concerning individuals or otherwise significantly affect them.

All processing is limited to what is necessary for these operational, analytical and reporting purposes, and is carried out in accordance with applicable data protection legislation.

## Processed data

Once you have accepted analytics, Matomo processes the following data each time you view a page:

- Your IP address. To protect your privacy, its last 2 bytes are anonymised.
- The date and time of the request.
- The title of the page you are viewing (page title).
- The address of the page you are viewing (page URL).
- The address of the page you came from (referrer URL), which shows whether you arrived from a search engine, a social network, another website, or by entering the address directly.
- The time in your local time zone.
- How long you spend on each page. To measure this more accurately, your browser sends a short signal to Matomo every 15 seconds or so while the page is in view.
- The main language of your browser (Accept-Language header).
- The user agent of your browser (User-Agent header), which describes your browser and operating system.
- A random visitor ID and the time of your first visit, kept in a cookie so Matomo can tell a returning visitor from a new one.

A page view is recorded when a page loads, and again when the address changes without a new page loading, for example when you follow a link to another section of the same page.

Matomo also records when you accept analytics, and when you opt out after having accepted, so we can see how visitors choose.

Matomo does not collect your screen resolution or browser plugins, and it does not record which links you click or which files you download.

For further information on how this data is used and secured, or to ask what data we hold about you, see "How to contact us" below.

## Cookies and browser storage

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. For general information about cookies, see [aboutcookies.org](https://www.aboutcookies.org) or [allaboutcookies.org](https://www.allaboutcookies.org).

Matomo sets its cookies only after you accept analytics, and deletes them if you opt out:

- `_pk_id` holds a random visitor ID and the time of your first visit. It expires after 13 months.
- `_pk_ses` marks a visit in progress. It expires after 30 minutes without activity.
- `_pk_ref` holds the address of the website you came from, when you arrive from another website. It expires after 6 months.

Two more cookies remember your analytics choice:

- `mtm_consent` is set when you accept analytics.
- `mtm_consent_removed` is set when you opt out. While it is present, Matomo sends nothing about your visits.

Setting one removes the other. Each holds only the time you made your choice, not an identifier, and expires 1 year after you made it; the banner then asks you again. All of these are first-party cookies used only on this website. Some browsers delete cookies sooner; Safari, for example, removes them after 7 days without a visit.

If your browser blocks cookies, your choice cannot be saved and Matomo records nothing.

This website also keeps two preferences in your browser's storage: whether you use the light or dark theme, and whether you have closed the announcement banner at the top of the page. These stay on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else.

## Your choice

On your first visit, a banner at the bottom of the page asks whether you accept analytics. Matomo records nothing until you accept, and the banner stays until you make a choice. After that, you can change your choice at any time with the setting below.

<AnalyticsPreference />

If you opt out, Matomo stops collecting data about your visits and deletes its cookies, to the extent technically feasible. Your choice applies to the browser and device you make it on.

If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, Matomo records nothing and the setting above cannot be changed. Matomo also needs JavaScript, so with JavaScript turned off nothing about your visits is collected.

Opting out has no adverse consequences. It does not affect your ability to access, use or benefit from the services and content on this website, which work the same whether or not your visits are included in the statistics.

## Hosting and third-party content

The pages of this website are hosted on [GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/), a service of GitHub, Inc., and delivered to you at elixir.no. [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) describes how GitHub handles data in providing this service.

Fonts, videos and the search index are served from this website itself. Some pages also load content from other services. When they do, your browser connects to that service directly, which gives it your IP address and the standard technical details of the request. That provider's own privacy policy applies:

- **Maps.** The pages for each partner institution under [About](/about) show a map from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/). See the [OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).
- **Training listings.** The [Training](/training) page loads upcoming events, past events and training materials from [ELIXIR TeSS](https://tess.elixir-europe.org/).
- **Service status.** The [Services](/services) and [Service status](/services/status) pages check whether each service is available through a small status service that we run on [Vercel](https://vercel.com/). If it cannot be reached, your browser contacts each service's website directly. See [Vercel's privacy notice](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-notice).
- **Images in older news articles.** Two news articles show an image hosted elsewhere, one by [Digital Life Norway](https://www.digitallifenorway.org/) and one by the [Data Stewardship Wizard](https://ds-wizard.org/) project.

## Transfers to third countries

The analytics data collected by Matomo is hosted by the [Norwegian Research and Education Cloud (NREC)](https://www.nrec.no/) within Norway. Appropriate technical and organisational measures are in place to maintain the security, confidentiality, integrity and availability of the data throughout its lifecycle.

The analytics data is stored and processed within Norway and is not transferred to, stored in, or otherwise processed in any foreign jurisdiction. The Consortium is committed to carrying out all processing in accordance with applicable data protection legislation, information security requirements, and established governance procedures.

The providers listed under "Hosting and third-party content" above handle data under their own policies and may process it outside Norway.

## Retention period

Personal data collected through Matomo is retained in raw form for 2 years from the date of collection. This period enables analysis of medium to long-term usage trends and supports service evaluation and reporting, including reporting associated with funding, performance assessment and strategic planning.

When the retention period expires, raw personal data is deleted or otherwise made unavailable for further analysis. Aggregated data may be retained indefinitely for historical, analytical, reporting and service improvement purposes. It is kept in a form that poses no privacy or security risk to data subjects.

## Your rights

Subject to the conditions and limitations set out in applicable data protection legislation, you have the following rights:

- **Right of access:** you can ask us at any time for a copy of your personal data.
- **Right to rectification:** you can ask us at any time to correct or complete information you believe is inaccurate.
- **Right to erasure:** you can ask us to delete the personal data we process about you, under certain conditions.
- **Right to data portability:** you can ask us to transfer the data we have collected about you to another organisation, or directly to you, under certain conditions.
- **Right to restrict processing:** you can ask us to restrict the processing of your personal data, under certain conditions.
- **Right to object:** you can object to the processing of analytics data about your visits at any time by opting out with the setting under "Your choice" above.

To exercise these rights, see "How to contact us" below.

## Right to lodge a complaint

If you believe that the processing of your personal data does not comply with applicable data protection legislation, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

In Norway, this is the [Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet)](https://www.datatilsynet.no/en/). You may also contact the data protection authority in the country where you live or work, or where the alleged infringement took place.

This right applies without prejudice to any other administrative or judicial remedies available to you under applicable law.
